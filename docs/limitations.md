# Limitations and safety boundary

This is an experimental port tested on one Xiaomi BE3600 2.5G **RD15**. It is not an official OpenWrt target or a universal BE3600 image. Check the hardware ID before using any part of the installation procedure.

- The **installed** image does not enable the 2.5 GbE port: its DTS omits PPE port 1 and its kernel has `CONFIG_MOTORCOMM_PHY` disabled. A separate RAM-only branch bound this unit's YT8821 at MDIO `0x07`, negotiated both 2.5 and 1 Gb/s, and reached 2.35 Gb/s in a short local TCP test. That branch has not been installed or qualified for sustained traffic; its 2.5 GbE USB test partner failed during a longer run. The three 1 GbE LAN ports work in the installed image.
- Existing installations of this port can use its guarded RD15 sysupgrade path through LuCI, tested in Chrome with configuration preservation and readback. This is not a stock-to-OpenWrt method or Attended Sysupgrade. The generated `factory.ubi` remains unqualified. Initial installation still requires the isolated RAM/UART procedure; see [upgrading.md](upgrading.md).
- Stock U-Boot's `bootmiwifi` is needed. Booting a FIT directly with `bootm` triggered an HLOS panic on the tested unit, including with an unmodified Xiaomi FIT.
- The original Xiaomi firmware stays in slot 1 only if that slot is intact on **your** device. Verify it before changing slot 0. This repository cannot restore calibration (`ART`), bootloader or board data.
- Wi-Fi uses a mix of the base tree's root-PD firmware and 5 GHz files extracted from the owner's Xiaomi firmware. The mix works on the tested unit; it is not a general firmware compatibility guarantee.
- RAM is 256 MiB, with large reserved regions. About 35 MiB remained available during a short load test with both radios. Avoid assuming that extra services will fit.
- The isolated 2.4 GHz test used one MediaTek MT7922 client. With that client's Bluetooth enabled, its Wi-Fi fell to one spatial stream and throughput varied widely. A Samsung phone later reached 126/81.6 Mb/s in a household Internet Speedtest on 2.4 GHz; this is a different workload and does not establish general client compatibility.
- Ethernet flow control fixed a router-to-PC bottleneck on one link. With multiple Ethernet clients, pause frames could delay unrelated traffic; this has not been measured.
- Forwarded peak throughput, HE/OFDMA behavior under the conservative AQL fallback, multi-client load, WPA3, MLO and bidirectional FT roaming remain unqualified. A short bridged TCP application test and household observations do not establish these properties.
- During the first household deployment, a 5 GHz client authenticated but initially received no DHCP offer over the bridge, although the gateway sent one. Temporarily removing and rejoining the unplugged LAN ports restored forwarding. The failure **recurred** with a 2.4 GHz client after a successful cold boot, and cycling the 1 GbE uplink restored its path. A phone later saw repeated DHCP offers for roughly 90 seconds before receiving an ACK on 5 GHz. Those October 13 observations did not identify a cause. Later controlled tests isolated a stale YT921x FDB entry and the current patch corrected that path; FT failures and other client behavior must be assessed separately. See [ap-deployment.md](ap-deployment.md).
- No public binary is provided: locally built images include proprietary Wi-Fi files. Do not commit or upload build outputs.

If you cannot reach a U-Boot prompt, boot the RAM image, or identify your flash layout exactly, stop before writing flash. See [recovery-and-rollback.md](recovery-and-rollback.md).

- A TX descriptor exhaustion burst and a mac80211 airtime warning were observed during household use. The airtime path is corrected; exhaustion gained counters, not a proven root-cause fix. A pairwise key-install ENOENT was measured immediately before FT association, but successful association alone does not establish uninterrupted client traffic.
- A partial NAND boot-environment write was encountered during the first updated boot. Page-sized writes with readback passed in the final image and restored boot-success marking. Repeated cold boots, power interruption and broad NAND endurance remain unqualified. See [the update record](update-2026-10-26.md).
